# Typosaurus promo — treatment

Style: Lemo-Opuscar *Living Screencast* grammar (real product UI, cursor = the user, push-ins on the action),
with one deliberate break: the README screenshots appear as-is, because the brief asked for them.

## Structures considered
1. **Before/after** — messy text, one tap, clean text, then features. *Chosen*: the hook lands in 3 s and every later beat is one feature, one line.
2. **A day of typing** — morning to night in one chat. Needs invented conversations; too long for 30 s.
3. **A list that turns** — eight shortcut chips flip into the logo. Strong ending, weak on settings and providers.

## Logline and arc
Fast thumbs make messy text; Typosaurus fixes it in one tap, then shows it is yours to shape.
Setup (typos) → turn (✨ fix, shortcuts, /fix) → ending (every screen at once, pulled into the logo).

## Camera
Push-in on the chip (2 s), push to the panel (5 s), push to the field (8 s), pull to the providers screen (11 s),
small reframes per feature, a scale-reveal pull back to the gallery (≈24 s), then a whip-pan with motion blur to the end card.

## Sound
explainroo `upbeat` underscore, ducked 8 dB under the Kokoro voice (`af_heart`, speed 1.05), accented on every chapter.
Foley from explainroo: clicks on every cursor tap, typing burst, sparkle on each fix, swipes on screen changes,
a riser into the near-silence before the end card, chime on the logo. Mix normalised to −14 LUFS.
